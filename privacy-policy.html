🛡 Privacy Policy

last updated: august 2026 · effective forever

The short version

We collect almost nothing. The quantum-safe network does not want your data — it wants your vigilance.

What we store

  • Display names you type in chat or on the board (stored locally in your browser, sent with public posts).
  • Messages and posts you publish publicly. They are public. Anyone can read them.
  • Local preferences (icon order, wallpaper choices, easter egg progress) stay in your browser's localStorage and never leave it.
  • Server logs of standard web requests, kept briefly for stability.

What we never do

  • No trackers, no analytics beacons, no ad networks.
  • No wallet connect. We cannot see your balances unless you vote on-chain, which is public by design.
  • No selling data. Your information is not ours to sell.

Third-party content

External links point to independent file hosts governed by their own policies. Embedded charts load from third-party services only after contract addresses are announced.

Your control

Clear your browser storage and everything local is gone. Ask nicely on the board and we will remove specific messages you regret posting.
