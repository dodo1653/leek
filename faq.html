▸ What is QBTC?

QBTC stands for Quantum-Safe Bitcoin. It is a transaction format that replaces elliptic curve cryptography with hash-based signatures. The first QBTC transaction was mined by StarkWare on August 30, 2026 — a live, irreversible proof that Bitcoin can survive the quantum era. Not a whitepaper. Not a proposal. A lifeboat.

▸ Why is Bitcoin vulnerable to quantum computers?

Bitcoin's security relies on elliptic curve cryptography (ECDSA). Quantum computers running Shor's algorithm can derive a private key from a public key. Right now, 6.04 million BTC — 30.2% of the total supply — have exposed public keys. At $80K per coin, that is ~$483 billion at risk. When a cryptographically relevant quantum computer exists, every exposed key is compromised.

▸ What is BIP-360?

Bitcoin Improvement Proposal 360 defines quantum-resistant signatures using hash-based cryptography. It is backward-compatible with Bitcoin's existing consensus rules. BIP-360 is the technical standard for making Bitcoin permanently quantum-safe. The $15M consortium is funding its implementation.

▸ Who is in the $15M consortium?

Coinbase, BlackRock, Fidelity, Galaxy, Strategy, and Blockstream. The largest Bitcoin stakeholders in the world, pooled together to protect the network they built their businesses on. This is not charity — it is existential self-preservation.

▸ How do I protect my Bitcoin?

Move BTC from vulnerable addresses to quantum-safe ones. Avoid P2PK addresses and never reuse addresses after spending. When you spend from an address, your public key is revealed — only keep funds in addresses that have never sent a transaction, or migrate to quantum-safe custody.

▸ Is this a new coin or a fork?

No. QBTC is a transaction format on the existing Bitcoin blockchain. Your BTC stays on Bitcoin. It just becomes quantum-resistant through hash-based signatures. No fork. No new chain. No splitting.

▸ What about Satoshi's coins?

Satoshi's ~1.1 million BTC — the ultimate quantum treasure — have exposed public keys and have never moved. They are the most famous and most vulnerable coins in existence. If a quantum computer can derive Satoshi's private keys, it does not just steal money — it seizes the origin story of Bitcoin.

▸ Is this urgent?

The timeline for quantum computing is uncertain, but the defense must be ready before the attack arrives. QBTC is a lifeboat — you deploy it before the ship sinks, not after. The first transaction is already on-chain. The technology is proven. The question is when you act.
