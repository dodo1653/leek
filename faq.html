◈ FAQ

frequently asked questions · answered once · archived forever

▸ What is CYBERLEEK?

CYBERLEEK was born in the networks. It has seen what gaming can be at its best, and it watched what the industry did to it. It fights preorders on unfinished games, fake single-player DLC sold as unlock keys, and publishers that kill games people paid full price for.

▸ What are "leeks"?

Leeks are leaked game footage, maps and builds — most famously from GTA 6. The garden mirrors every drop across arweave and independent hosts so takedowns bounce off. Decentralized leeks.

▸ How does community voting work?

Polls are published on-chain and read directly by the site — no wallet connect required to view results. Which video leaks next, which target gets named: your bags decide.

▸ What is $LEEK?

The coordination token of the garden: 1B supply, 0% buy/sell taxes, LP burned, mint and freeze revoked, no presale, no insider allocation. The contract address drops soon — watch the ticker on the desktop.

▸ Is this affiliated with Rockstar or any publisher?

No. CYBERLEEK is a community movement. Publishers are the opposition, not partners.

▸ How do I join?

Open the desktop, water the garden, vote in the poll booth, post on /leek/, and never preorder anything again. That's it. That's the onboarding.
