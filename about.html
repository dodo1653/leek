⚛ QBTC

quantum-safe bitcoin · the first lifeboat · mined aug 30 2026

On August 30, 2026, StarkWare mined the first quantum-resistant Bitcoin transaction. This is not a whitepaper. This is not a proposal. This is a transaction on the Bitcoin blockchain, live and irreversible. The age of quantum vulnerability is over. The age of quantum defense has begun.

Bitcoin's elliptic curve cryptography — the backbone of every transaction since 2009 — is vulnerable to quantum computers. When sufficiently powerful quantum machines arrive, every exposed public key becomes a target. That includes 6.04 million BTC — 30.2% of the total supply — sitting in addresses with visible public keys. At $80K per coin, that is ~$483 billion at risk.

QBTC changes this. By replacing elliptic curve signatures with quantum-safe hashing, QBTC proves that Bitcoin can survive the quantum era without breaking the chain.

What Is QBTC?

QBTC stands for Quantum-Safe Bitcoin. It is not a new coin. It is not a fork. It is a transaction format that uses hashing instead of elliptic curve cryptography to prove ownership. The first QBTC transaction was mined by StarkWare as a demonstration that the technology works — a lifeboat on the Bitcoin sea.

Think of it as an emergency escape hatch. You do not use a lifeboat for a casual cruise, but when the ship is sinking, it is the only thing that matters. QBTC is for moving vulnerable Bitcoin into quantum-safe custody before the threat materializes.

Why It Matters

6.04 million BTC are exposed. That is 30.2% of Bitcoin's total supply — roughly $483 billion at $80K — sitting in addresses where the public key is visible on the blockchain. If a quantum computer can derive a private key from a public key, all of it is gone.

And it is not just random wallets. Satoshi Nakamoto's coins — approximately 1.1 million BTC mined in Bitcoin's earliest days — are the ultimate quantum treasure. If anyone can break those keys, they hold the keys to Bitcoin's origin story.

The $15M Consortium

The problem is too big for one company. A consortium of Coinbase, BlackRock, Fidelity, Galaxy, Strategy, and Blockstream has pooled $15 million to accelerate quantum-resistant infrastructure. This is not charity. This is survival. Every major Bitcoin stakeholder has a reason to ensure quantum computers do not destroy the network they built their businesses on.

BIP-360

Bitcoin Improvement Proposal 360 is the technical standard for making quantum resistance permanent. QBTC is the proof that it works. BIP-360 defines how quantum-safe signatures integrate with Bitcoin's existing consensus rules — backward compatible, forward-looking, and built to last.

The Lifeboat Doctrine

QBTC is not for everyday transactions. It is a lifeboat — an emergency mechanism for moving BTC from vulnerable addresses to quantum-safe ones. When the quantum threat becomes real, there will be no time for debates. The lifeboat launches when the ship is already taking water. Use it before you need it, or do not use it at all.

Final Word

The first QBTC transaction is a message to Bitcoin holders: the threat is real, the solution exists, and the clock is ticking. Satoshi's coins are the ultimate prize. 6.04 million BTC are exposed. The consortium has committed the funds. BIP-360 is the standard. All that remains is action.

This is QBTC. The lifeboat is launched.
