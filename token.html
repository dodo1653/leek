What $BTC does

$BTC is the coordination layer for the quantum-resistant Bitcoin movement. It represents the shift from elliptic curve cryptography to quantum-safe hashing — the same shift StarkWare demonstrated on August 30, 2026 when they mined the first QBTC transaction.

The quantum threat

Every Bitcoin transaction since 2009 has relied on elliptic curve cryptography. This works today. It will not work when quantum computers can factor large enough numbers. 6.04 million BTC — 30.2% of the supply — have exposed public keys. At $80K, that is $483 billion waiting to be claimed.

The consortium

Coinbase, BlackRock, Fidelity, Galaxy, Strategy, and Blockstream have committed $15 million to quantum-resistant infrastructure. This is not a meme. This is the financial industry protecting itself from an existential threat.

The lifeboat

QBTC is not for daily use. It is an emergency mechanism — a lifeboat for moving BTC from vulnerable addresses to quantum-safe ones before quantum computers arrive. The first transaction is already on-chain. The technology is proven. The question is timing.
